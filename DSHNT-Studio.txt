THE STORY
---------
Most people spend their first salary on themselves. Dishant spent his
on a DSLR. Photography has always been the thing he loves more than
anything, and that first camera turned a passion into a craft.
Today DSHNT Studio is where that passion becomes his full-time work:
every frame is taken by someone who chose the camera before anything
else.

Tagline ideas:
  - "My first salary bought a camera. The rest is history."
  - "Passion first. Pictures always."
  - "Every frame, from the heart."

PART 2 - HOW TO SHOW DSHNT STUDIO TO MORE PEOPLE
==================================================

ONLINE PRESENCE
  1.  Instagram first - post consistently (3-5 times a week): best shots,
      carousels, before/after edits, behind-the-scenes.
  2.  Instagram Reels - short "how I got this shot" and BTS videos;
      these reach far more people than photos alone.
  3.  Tell the story - a pinned post/reel about buying the first DSLR
      with the first salary. People connect with real stories.
  4.  Portfolio website - a simple site with galleries, pricing, and a
      contact/booking form (this repo can become that website!).
  5.  Google Business Profile - so people find "photographer near me"
      and can leave reviews.
  6.  YouTube - editing tutorials, shoot vlogs, camera tips.
  7.  Pinterest - great for wedding, fashion and food photos.
  8.  Facebook page + local community groups.
  9.  WhatsApp Business - catalogue of packages, quick replies,
      status updates with recent work.
  10. Behance / 500px / Unsplash - reach the creative community.

LOCAL & OFFLINE
  11. Free or discounted shoots for friends and family early on to
      build the portfolio and get testimonials.
  12. Partner with local businesses - cafés, salons, boutiques, gyms:
      shoot their content in exchange for credit and referrals.
  13. Tie up with wedding planners, decorators, makeup artists and
      event venues for referrals.
  14. Photo exhibition - display best prints at a café, college or
      community hall.
  15. Visiting cards and a QR code that opens the Instagram/website.
  16. Watermark / signature "DSHNT Studio" subtly on shared photos.
  17. Free mini-shoots at local festivals and events
      (e.g. a Diwali or Holi photo booth).
  18. Photowalks - organise group photowalks in the city.
  19. Workshops for beginners at schools or colleges.

GROWTH IDEAS
  20. Ask every happy client for a review and permission to share.
  21. Referral offer - discount for clients who bring a friend.
  22. Collaborate with local influencers, models and creators.
  23. Enter photography contests and share any wins.
  24. Seasonal offers - Valentine's couple shoots, festive family
      packages, back-to-school portraits.
  25. Monthly themed series (e.g. "Faces of the City", "Street Stories")
      to keep followers coming back.
  26. Sell prints, calendars or photo presets online.
  27. Consistent branding - same logo, colours and editing style
      everywhere so people recognise DSHNT Studio instantly.
